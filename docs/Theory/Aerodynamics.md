## Scope of Aerodynamics

To construct a high-fidelity digital twin in **Voyager Sim**, we must model forces beyond simple rigid body dynamics. Key topics to be detailed here include:

1. **Rotor Physics & Blade Element Theory (BET)**: Modeling lift and drag forces generated by rotor blade segments rotating through the air, rather than assuming static thrust coefficients ($k_f$).
2. **Induced Flow & Ground Effect**: The increase in thrust and change in aerodynamic efficiency experienced by the quadcopter when flying close to the ground (ground boundary layer interaction).
3. **Parasitic and Induced Drag**: Modeling translational drag on the vehicle frame and blades, including torque overhead ($k_m$) scaling at higher speeds.
4. **Gyroscopic & Precession Moments**: Torques exerted on the frame due to high-speed spinning motor rotors subjected to pitch and roll angular rates.
5. **Wind and Wind Shear Models**: Incorporating static wind vectors, gusts (Dryden or Von Karman turbulence models), and wind shear gradients into the simulation.
6. **Blade Flapping**: Modeling the asymmetric lift forces experienced by advancing and retreating rotor blades during high forward-speed flight, resulting in rotor plane tilt.
